About Orchid Society

Orchid Co-operative Housing Society Limited is a residential community located within the Mahavir Kalpavruksha township at Kasarvadawali, Thane. Established in 2023, the society is home to a vibrant community of families and professionals who share a commitment to safe, comfortable, and harmonious living.


This portal serves as a central platform for residents to access notices, submit complaints, stay informed about community activities, and connect with the society committee.
