Who We Are

About Orchid Society

Orchid Co-operative Housing Society Limited is a proud residential community nestled within the prestigious Mahavir Kalpavruksha township by DSS Group — located in the heart of Kasarvadawali, Ghodbunder Road, Thane West.

Established in 2023, we are home to a thriving community of families and professionals committed to safe, harmonious, and comfortable living. The Orchid tower is part of a large integrated township offering 1 BHK and 2 BHK apartments with modern design and top-quality specifications.

This portal is our residents' central hub — for notices, complaint registration, event updates, and connecting with the managing committee.
