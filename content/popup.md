# Pop-up Notice

> This file controls the popup that shows when residents open the website.
>
> ─────────────────────────────────────────────
>
> ### How to post a notice
>
> 1. Edit the section below the marker line.
> 2. Set `title:` (required), `icon:` (an emoji), and `accent:` (color).
> 3. Write the body. Each blank line starts a new paragraph.
> 4. Lines that look like `Label: Value` automatically format as a
>    highlighted info row (great for timings, dates, contact info).
> 5. Commit the change. Site updates in ~30 seconds.
>
> ### How to clear the popup (no popup shown)
>
> Replace the entire content section below with just the word `none` and
> commit. Or delete it. Either way, no popup will appear.
>
> ### Available accents (color theme of the popup)
>
> - `blue`   – utility info: water, electricity, internet
> - `green`  – general announcements (default)
> - `gold`   – deadlines, heads-up
> - `red`    – urgent, safety, security
>
> ### Common icons
>
> 💧 water · ⚡ power · 📶 internet · 🚧 maintenance · 🪔 event · 🔥 safety
> 📢 announcement · 💰 payment · 📅 deadline · 🚨 emergency · 🎉 celebration
>
> ### Formatting tips
>
> - **Bold:** wrap text in `**double asterisks**`
> - *Italic:* wrap text in `*single asterisks*`
> - [Link to a page](https://example.com): use `[label](url)`
> - Info rows: write a line as `Label: value` (e.g. `Morning: 6:00 AM`)
> - To skip the popup without deleting the example, change the section
>   marker from `POPUP CONTENT START` to anything else.
>

title: Water Supply Update
icon: 🚰
accent: blue

Hi All,

We will be releasing water at 7:00 PM. Our intention is to keep the water supply running continuously instead of shutting it off after a fixed duration. However, this will only be possible with the support and cooperation of all residents.

For today, we request everyone to use water judiciously:

Please keep taps closed when not in use.
Avoid running washing machines and dishwashers if possible.
Refrain from any heavy or non-essential water usage.

Let's all work together and enjoy uninterrupted water supply.

However, if we observe excessive water consumption today, we may have no option but to shut off the water supply within an hour to ensure fair distribution for everyone.
